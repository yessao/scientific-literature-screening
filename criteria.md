# Inclusion criteria: superconductivity and specific heat, 2000-2001

- **Date:** published in 2000 or 2001 (the export gives the year only).
- **Topic:** superconductivity, in any material or model, theoretical or experimental.
- **Specific-heat score:**
  - **3, central:** specific heat or heat capacity is the main subject of the paper.
  - **2, substantial:** C(T) is measured or computed as key evidence, for example to determine the gap, the jump at Tc or the Sommerfeld coefficient.
  - **1, minor:** specific heat is mentioned briefly or is a side result.
  - **0, not relevant:** not about a superconductor, or about another property (for example thermal conductivity or resistivity) or another system.
- **Type:** experimental, theoretical, both, or unclear.
